Privacy Policy

1. No data collection

ReSync does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The developer operates no servers that receive your information. ReSync contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking, and no crash-reporting services that identify you.

2. Data stored on your device

Anything ReSync creates is stored locally on your device only and never leaves it, including:

  • App settings and preferences (themes, fonts, layout)
  • Subscribed and favorited subreddits, saved posts, and drafts
  • Cached posts, images, and media for faster loading and offline reading
  • Search and browsing history kept only for the app's own convenience features
  • Your Reddit login session token, if you choose to sign in, stored in the device's secure storage

You can erase all of this at any time by clearing the cache from settings, logging out, or uninstalling the app.

3. Communication with Reddit

When you use ReSync, the app communicates directly with Reddit to fetch content and perform the actions you request. Your use of Reddit through this app is governed by Reddit's own Privacy Policy and User Agreement. ReSync has no control over, and takes no responsibility for, Reddit's data practices.

4. Third-party content

Posts and comments may contain links or embedded media hosted by third parties (for example image, video, or link hosts). Opening that content connects you to those services, whose own privacy policies then apply. ReSync does not control them.

5. Children's privacy

ReSync does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. Any changes are delivered with the app itself and shown on this screen; the "last updated" date above reflects the current version.
